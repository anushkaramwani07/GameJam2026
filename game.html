Unleash The Beast: Rise of the WereCheetah

The attack came on the night of the new moon, the one night when the Werecheetahs were most vulnerable. I awoke to the piercing cry of an alarm and frantic shouts warning that we were under attack. Bursting from my hut, I found the tribe in complete chaos as everyone rushed to escape. Somewhere amidst the panic, I was separated from the others. Now, a hunter is pursuing me through the darkness, and my mission is simple: stay alive

How to Play:

- Press SPACE to jump over the hunter

- Earn points each time you successfully avoid the hunter

- Watch Out! The hunter gets faster every 2 points you score

- See how long you can survive and achieve your highest score!
